Our Core Values

Client centric

We believe in the value of relationships. We view every client relationship as a partnership, and truly believe that our success is a result of our client’s success.

Best People

We believe that our people are our best assets. We pledge to attract and retain the best and brightest professionals who will be rewarded, recognized and respected for their contributions to the firm.

One Company, One Team

We are committed to the one company, one team approach. We believe that by operating as one company, rather than a group of sole practitioners sharing an office, we will deliver the best possible client service.

Excellence & Competence

Double Pillars adheres only to the highest levels of excellence, competence and performance. This includes performing high-quality work that is designed to share knowledge and deliver value to our clients.

Integrity & Honesty

Integrity and honesty are the foundation of our profession. We place professional responsibility above personal gain. Our clients can trust us to keep our promises and manage our business with the highest level of integrity and honesty.

Community

At Double Pillars we believe in giving back to the community, and truly believe that when the community prospers, we also prosper.

Accountability

We are accountable for our actions that influence the lives of our clients and fellow workers. We believe that by being accountable; we accomplish more and deliver exceptional services.
