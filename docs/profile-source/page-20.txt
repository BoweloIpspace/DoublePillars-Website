Our People

The most critical component of any successful business relationship is a highly qualified and committed team of professionals who thoroughly understand the business of their clients and can translate that knowledge into value. Double Pillars Consultants is a multi-racial service organisation which at present has 7 highly qualified and experienced employees, who are stationed in various capacities within the firm. The firm also offers opportunities to its semi qualified employees to further their studies and gain practical experience in accounting, finance, tax and corporate secretarial. Apart from focusing only on the strategic aspects of Double Pillars, management is also actively involved in efforts to manage stakeholder relationships, achieve maximum productivity and motivate staff. This has naturally given the firm a flat organisational structure which also enables the key management to be involved in controlling the business effectively. Double Pillars strives to hire people who take pride in their work. The firm’s philosophy is based on responsibility and mutual respect. Double Pillars maintains an environment and structure that encourages productivity and respect for clients and its employees.

Quality Statement

We have internal processes to effectively monitor and control quality right from project planning, implementation and review. In the delivery of any assignment, we affirm our strong and unwavering stand in the upholding of the ethical values of the firm. In this regards, we believe all services are to be delivered with the right standard of measurable quality and in the manner that shall preserve the values of our clients. Our Quality policy is as follows:

To build a solid base with flexible policies to create an environment for innovation and development.

To deepen the meaning of quality to each member of the firm regardless of his/her job description.

To progress the standard level of commitment to customers in fulfilling their demands.
