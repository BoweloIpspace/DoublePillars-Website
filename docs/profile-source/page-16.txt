Corporate structuring and re-organization

We often encounter groups or companies that, due to the ad-hoc nature of their evolution, suffer an irrelevant or even cumbersome corporate structure. The proliferation of companies within a group often leads to inefficiencies and needless expense. For example, copious statutory returns, undue demands upon management and treasury and the inability to set losses off against profits can draw heavily on any groups human and financial resources. We deliver an expert service in the restructuring of corporate groups in order to maximize efficiency. We are particularly skilled in utilizing the complex ‘corporate rules’ to affect restructuring without creating CGT and income tax exposures.
