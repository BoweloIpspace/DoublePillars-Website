About Us

Double Pillars Holding (Proprietary) Limited is a Botswana registered company incorporated in 2014 (BW00000094251) under the Botswana Companies Act to pursue the business of providing professional services to corporates and individuals in Botswana and the region in general. The company carries business under the trading name Double Pillars Consultants with its head office in Gaborone.

The firm specializes in Forensic Accounting, Financial and Management Accounting, Business advisory services, tax and tax planning, and corporate secretarial services. The firm is registered and recognized as a practicing member firm with the Botswana Institute of Chartered Accountants (BICA), with registration no. MeFBW16028. BICA is the body that regulates the accounting and auditing profession in Botswana.
