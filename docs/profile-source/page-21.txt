Corporate Social Responsibility Statement

We strive to implement Botswana’s “Training and Localization Programme”.

Our Managing Partner’s track record demonstrates our commitment to training and development of citizen accountants in Botswana.

We train citizens for our clients, apart from placing suitable candidates at appropriate levels in their organizations.

We are committed to the sustained growth in number and development in quality of citizen entrepreneurs that we offer free services for the deserving candidates.

At Double Pillars, we value and respect the diversity of the interests of our various stakeholder groups. Employees’ welfare is at the heart of our human resources policy. Workers have an opportunity to develop a sense of security in their jobs as they sign long term contracts with Double Pillars. Training and development is also offered internally to equip employees with the skills that enable them to perform effectively in their current appointments and still help them in their careers after Double Pillars. Double Pillars is a place where issues of health and safety in the workplace receive priority attention to minimise accidents at work. The positive attitude of management towards this matter has been very instrumental in setting a tone where employees are safety conscious.

We also respect the markets and communities in which we operate with a view to preserving their values and environments. The resources we use in our business are environmentally friendly. Management is involved in activities to assess on a continuous basis the environmental footsteps of our activities.
