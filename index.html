About Us

DoublePillars Consultants

Double Pillars Holding (Proprietary) Limited is a Botswana-registered company incorporated in 2014 under the Botswana Companies Act (registration no. BW00000094251), trading as Double Pillars Consultants. With its head office in Gaborone, the firm provides professional services to corporates and individuals in Botswana and the region.

Our Approach

Professional support, grounded in sound judgement.

Our official service scope covers Forensic Accounting, Financial and Management Accounting, Business Advisory Services, Tax and Tax Planning, and Corporate Secretarial Services. The firm is registered and recognized as a practicing member firm with the Botswana Institute of Chartered Accountants (BICA), registration no. MeFBW16028; BICA regulates the accounting and auditing profession in Botswana.
