Business Accounting

Business Accounting

Financial and management accounting including records, transactions, bank reconciliations, management accounts, PAYE and VAT returns, annual statements, budgets and monitoring actual results against budgets.

Accounting & Management Services

Staying on top of accounting is a challenge for business caught up in the daily priorities of running a business. Apart from keeping you abreast of the precise financial state of your business, our accounting service will keep you compliant with ever-more complex tax, statutory audit and company responsibilities. We provide both ad-hoc and permanent ongoing support to companies, owner-managed businesses, trusts, partnerships, individuals and businesses in Botswana with assistance in:

  • Regular maintenance of financial records: processing financial transactions, bank reconciliations, management accounts, dealing with your statutory returns (PAYE, VAT etc.) and the setting up of systems by your designated accounts clerk.
  • Annual requirements: financial statement, management of supporting documentation and income tax returns.
  • Preparation of budgets and monthly management accounts and the monitoring of actual results against budgets.
  • Keeping accurate and reliable accounts, providing more cost-effective ways by helping you avoid penalties.

We also have staff constantly on hand to assist you with queries and problems whenever they arise and can even train your bookkeeper, if required.

you can access your own cost-effective outsourced accounts division from us, either by short term agreement or a field monthly billing, agreed in advance.

All our financial statements are prepared using accounts preparation software called "case ware" this allows us to ensure that the financials are compliant with latest accounting standards and complient with IFRS either for SME's or full IFRS.

Forensic Services

We are Certified Forensic Investigation professionals with many years of experience in the field of forensic accounting, auditing and investigations.

Corporate fraud is on the rise. Fraud is deception, deliberately practiced in order to secure unfair or unlawful gain. Companies today do not just face risks from internal fraud but also from external criminals who are technologically savvy. The important role of forensic accounting plays in minimizing financials losses through fraud is fast gaining ground. Forensic accounting is the use of accounting, auditing, and investigative skills to assist in legal matters. It encompasses litigation support, investigation and dispute resolution.

We are professionals who use a unique blend of education and experience to apply accounting, auditing and investigative skills to uncover truth, form legal opinions, and assist in investigations.

Our skills set are:

  • Forensic accounting

  • Money laundering investigations

  • Preventive controls

Corporate Finance

The role of corporate finance is often played during critical times in the history of a business. Whether you are expanding, restructuring, seeking new market opportunities or disposing a business, you need a team of advisors with the broad experience required to achieve the best possible result for you. Double Pillars corporate finance team has the expertise that comes only from repeatedly completing successful transactions. this we've achieved for clients across a variety of sectors and for every size of enterprise- from owner managed businesses to multi-nationals. whatever your objective, we can mobilize a team that will bring the most effective level of expertise to the table with a commitment to achieving a successful outcome that matches, or even exceeds your expectations.

Our corporate finance offerings fall into four distinct areas of expertise:

  • Acquisitions
  • Capital raising
  • Valuations
  • Corporate structuring and re-organization

Acquisitions, disposals and mergers

  • Help with expanding in your current market.
  • Identifying target acquisitions.
  • Managing the acquisition process-whether buying a company, part of a company, its shares, trade or assets.
  • Intermediary support in buying and selling businesses.
  • Complete corporate acquisitions, Management Buy Out (MBO), Management Buy In (MBI) and Buy In together with a Management Buy Out (BIMBO).
  • Comprehensive due diligence investigations.
  • Disposals, including corporate divestment programmers, non-core business disposal and retirement sales.

Capital Raising

  • Advice on the right funding strategy for your business from owner managed businesses to large private companies and listed companies.
  • Selecting the right type of funding-whether debt, equity or a mix-and developing a tight proposition that is attractive to potential founders.
  • Structuring transactions, from debt and equity through to IPO's.

Valuations

We provide an objective and independent service that covers;

  • Business Valuations
  • Share Valuation
  • Indicative value exercises as part of lead advisory and transaction support services
  • Viability studies and capital investment appraisal

Corporate structuring and re-organization

We often encounter groups or companies that, due to the ad-hoc nature of their evolution, suffer an irrelevant or even cumbersome corporate structure. The proliferation of companies within a group often leads to inefficiencies and needless expense. For example, copious statutory returns, undue demands upon management and treasury and the inability to set losses off against profits can draw heavily on any groups human and financial resources. We deliver an expert service in the restructuring of corporate groups in order to maximize efficiency. We are particularly skilled in utilizing the complex ‘corporate rules’ to affect restructuring without creating CGT and income tax exposures.

  • Fair and reasonable evaluation and reports

  • Transaction support
