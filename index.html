About DoublePillars

A trusted partner for important business decisions.

Double Pillars Holding (Proprietary) Limited is a Botswana-registered company incorporated in 2014 under the Botswana Companies Act (registration no. BW00000094251), trading as Double Pillars Consultants. With its head office in Gaborone, the firm provides professional services to corporates and individuals in Botswana and the region.

DoublePillars is committed to personal attention, advanced training, technical expertise, financial acumen, timely professional service and trusted advisory relationships. Every client relationship is viewed as a partnership, with the firm’s success connected to the client’s success.
