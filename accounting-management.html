Accounting & Management Services

Staying on top of accounting is a challenge for business caught up in the daily priorities of running a business. Apart from keeping you abreast of the precise financial state of your business, our accounting service will keep you compliant with ever-more complex tax, statutory audit and company responsibilities.

How we support your business

We also have staff on standby to assist you with queries and problems whenever they arise and can even train your bookkeeper, if required.

You can access your own cost-effective outsourced accounts division from us, either by short term agreement or a field monthly billing, agreed in advance.

All our financial statements are prepared using accounts preparation software called “Caseware” that allows us to ensure that the financials are compliant with latest accounting standards and compliant with IFRS either for SMEs or full IFRS.

Our services include

  • Regular maintenance of financial records: processing financial transactions, bank reconciliations, management accounts, dealing with your statutory returns (PAYE, VAT etc.) and the setting up of systems by your designated accounts clerk.
  • Annual requirements: financial statement, management of supporting documentation and income tax returns.
  • Preparation of budgets and monthly management accounts and the monitoring of actual results against budgets.
  • Keeping accurate and reliable accounts, providing more cost-effective ways by helping you avoid penalties.
