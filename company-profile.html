About Us

Double Pillars Holding (Proprietary) Limited is a Botswana registered company incorporated in 2014 under the Botswana Companies Act to pursue the business of providing professional services to corporates and individuals in Botswana and the region in general. The company carries business under the trading name Double Pillars Consultants with its head office in Gaborone.

The firm specializes in Forensic Accounting, Financial and Management Accounting, Business advisory services, tax and tax planning, and corporate secretarial services. The firm is registered and recognized as a practicing member firm with the Botswana Institute of Chartered Accountants (BICA).

Our Vision

To be the preferred provider of outstanding professional accounting services to all our clients operating in various sectors of the economy in Botswana and in the region.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create value for our clients, our people and the firm by providing quality professional services. We will accomplish this by building a firm that will stand the test of time and by creating a cultural pride, passion and commitment that will enable us to continue to attract and retain the best and brightest professionals. We pledge to become our clients most trusted advisors.

Commitment

We are committed to providing our clients with assurance that the personal assistance they receive comes from years of advanced training, technical expertise and financial accuracy. By delivering high quality professional services on a timely basis, we have built a solid reputation as a trusted advisor.

Our People

The firm's philosophy is based on responsibility and mutual respect, and its aim is to provide an environment that encourages productivity and respect for clients and employees.

Quality Statement

We have internal processes to effectively monitor and control quality right from project planning, implementation and review. Our quality policy is to follow all relevant standards, deliver quality services and maintain a working environment that supports innovation and development.

The Right Fee

Our approach to fees is transparent and fair and reflects our understanding of the time of the commitment required of all members of our team throughout the engagement. Double Pillars requires no learning curve, every minute we charge will be productive time and will be reflected as such in the price as value added.

Corporate Social Responsibility

We strive to implement Botswana's “Training and Localization Programme”. Our Managing Partner's track record demonstrates our commitment to training and development of citizen accountants in Botswana. We train citizens for our clients, apart from placing suitable candidates at appropriate levels in their organizations. We are committed to the sustained growth in number and development in quality of citizen entrepreneurs that we offer free services for the deserving candidates.

At Double Pillars, we value and respect the diversity of the interests of our various stakeholder groups. Employees' welfare is at the heart of our human resources policy. Workers have an opportunity to develop a sense of security in their jobs as they sign long term contracts with Double Pillars. Training and development is also offered internally to equip employees with the skills that enable them to ensure they deliver current appointments and still help them in their careers after Double Pillars.

We also respect the markets and communities in which we operate with a view to preserving their values and environments. The resources we use in our business are environmentally friendly. Management is involved in activities to assess on a continuous basis the environmental footprints of our activities.
