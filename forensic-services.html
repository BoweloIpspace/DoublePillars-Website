Forensic Services

We are Certified Forensic Investigation professionals with many years of experience in the field of forensic accounting, auditing and investigations.

Corporate fraud is on the rise. Fraud is deception, deliberately practiced in order to secure unfair or unlawful gain. Companies today do not just face risks from internal fraud but also from external criminals who are technologically savvy. The important role of forensic accounting plays in minimizing financials losses through fraud is fast gaining ground. Forensic accounting is the use of accounting, auditing, and investigative skills to assist in legal matters. It encompasses litigation support, investigation and dispute resolution.

We are professionals who use a unique blend of education and experience to apply accounting, auditing and investigative skills to uncover truth, form legal opinions, and assist in investigations.

Our skills set are:

  • Private investigations
  • Criminal investigations
  • Fraud audit
  • Forensic accounting
  • Tracing hidden assets
  • Money laundering investigations
  • Expert witnessing
  • Preventive controls
  • Anti-fraud/corruption training
