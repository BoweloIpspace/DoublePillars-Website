Corporate Secretarial Services

You can save a lot of time and money, not to mention trouble, by engaging the services of a good company secretarial service.

We can help you with the maze of regulation and the myriad tasks required to conform with the rules of company ownership and administration. Our experienced professionals take care of any aspects of your company secretarial work so that you can remain safe in the knowledge that you remain compliant and your affairs remain in order.

Services we provide include;

  • Registration of companies and Ccs
  • Forming companies and close corporations (Ccs)
  • Off the shelf companies and Ccs
  • Changing directors of companies and members of Ccs
  • Company and CC conversions
  • Changing company and CC name
  • Submission of company annual duty
  • Registration of external companies
  • Increasing, converting, subdividing or consolidating share capital and the redemption and repurchase of shares
  • Making changes to memorandum and Articles of Associations
  • Providing compliance service to company secretaries
  • Helping clients with their AGM
  • Writing minutes for clients or review their drafts
  • Preparing documents to effect share transfers and allotment of shares
  • Taking care of the routine maintenance of statutory registers and dealing with returns of the Registrar of Companies
  • Change of financial year ends
  • Company secretarial audits
  • Registration of defensive names
  • Registration of changes of trustees of trusts.
